Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and processes

How do I request a quote for my project?

Requesting a quote is simple! You can fill out our contact form on the website, call us at +2348109154248, or visit our office. Our team will review your project requirements and provide a detailed quote within 48 hours.

What is the typical timeline for a construction project?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. Residential projects typically take 6-12 months, while commercial projects may take 12-24 months. We'll provide a detailed timeline during the initial consultation.

Do you offer warranty on completed projects?

Yes, we offer comprehensive warranties on all our projects. Structural elements typically come with a 10-year warranty, while other components have 1-5 year warranties depending on the type. Details are included in your contract.

How do you ensure quality control during construction?

Quality is our top priority. We implement rigorous quality control procedures including daily inspections, regular testing, material verification, and compliance checks. Our dedicated QA team ensures every aspect meets our high standards.

Are your projects environmentally friendly?

Sustainability is core to our values. We use eco-friendly materials, implement waste reduction programs, and follow green building standards. Many of our projects are LEED certified or use similar sustainable practices.

Do you handle permits and regulatory requirements?

Yes, we handle all permitting, regulatory compliance, and legal requirements. Our experienced team knows local building codes and regulations, ensuring smooth approval processes and compliance throughout the project.
